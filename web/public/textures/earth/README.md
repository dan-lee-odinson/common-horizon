## What it is

A cloud-free picture of the Earth's land, ice and sea floor for the month of November, built by
NASA from satellite data. It has no clouds and no night lights.

## Layout

Plate carrée (equirectangular): longitude 180° W at the left edge to 180° E at the right edge,
latitude 90° N at the top to 90° S at the bottom. The prime meridian runs down the middle.

## What was done to it

Resized with Pillow (Lanczos) and saved as progressive JPEG at quality 88. Nothing else: no color
changes, no cropping, no added detail.

Checked after resizing: the Sahara, the central Pacific, Greenland, the Amazon, the Australian
interior and Antarctica sit at their own longitudes and latitudes in the image.

SI/AI: prepared with Claude (Anthropic), Claude Fable 5.2, by script. The image itself is NASA's.
